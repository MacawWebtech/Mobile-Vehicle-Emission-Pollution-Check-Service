Two-Wheeler Emission Test

Bikes, scooters and mopeds — petrol or CNG. We test right at your gate or in your building's parking. From ₹149 · about 15 min.

Book a Doorstep Test

What we check

Readings are compared with the legal limits for your vehicle's fuel and emission standard (BS-III, BS-IV, BS-VI).

  • Carbon monoxide (CO) at idle
  • Unburnt fuel (HC) in ppm
  • Engine idle speed (RPM) check
  • Visual smoke check

How your visit goes

We confirm

You get the technician's name and a live tracking link.

We arrive

In your 30-minute slot, with ID and calibrated kit.

We test

Probe in the exhaust, readings on screen — about 15 min.

You're certified

Certificate on WhatsApp and email, same day.

What to keep ready

  • Registration certificate (RC) number
  • Vehicle parked with the exhaust reachable
  • Engine able to idle for 3–4 minutes

Privacy policy

We collect only what we need to test your vehicle and issue a certificate: your name, phone, address, vehicle number and test readings.

We share test results with the official vehicle database as required by law. We never sell your data.

You can download or delete your data anytime from Settings in your dashboard.

Terms of service

Bookings are confirmed once you receive an SMS or WhatsApp message with your technician's name.

The test result reflects your vehicle's condition at the time of testing. We cannot change a reading.

Please make sure the vehicle is parked where it can be safely tested.

Cancellations & refunds
